Pokémon Cards: Side Hustle and Rarest Finds

Pokémon cards make a great side hustle if you start small and learn the market. The basic idea is simple: buy cards for less than they're worth, then sell them for more. You can find cheap cards at card shows, in local trade groups, or by buying whole collections, then sort out the valuable ones and sell them on sites like eBay or TCGplayer. Check recent sold listings to see what a card really sells for, and keep track of what you spend so you know your profit. Getting a good card graded by a company like PSA, BGS or CGC can raise its price a lot, but grading costs money and takes time. Beware of fakes, and never spend money you can't afford to lose.

Some cards are famously rare. The most legendary is the Pikachu Illustrator, a 1998 promo card given to winners of an illustration contest in Japan, with only a few dozen ever made. A top-graded copy sold for over $5 million, the highest price ever paid for a Pokémon card. Other collector favorites include the 1st Edition Shadowless Charizard from the original Base Set, the Trophy Pikachu cards handed out at early tournaments, and rare prerelease cards that never reached stores. What makes a card valuable is how few exist, how good its condition is, and how much people want it.
